Resistance Band Row

Needs a resistance band
3sets
12reps

Start here, adjust as needed.

How to do it

Anchor a resistance band at roughly waist height (around a door handle or post). Hold one end in each hand and step back until there is tension in the band. Pull your elbows back toward your hips, squeezing your shoulder blades together at the end of the movement.
