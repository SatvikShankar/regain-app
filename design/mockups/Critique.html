Regain · redesign · 21 Oct 2026

Flow critique

Every function in the brief survives. What changes is how many steps the everyday actions take, and where the app shows people what they've already done. Each change below has its reason so it can be weighed before building.

Recommended changes
1

Home becomes a dashboard with one-tap logging

Logging happens several times a week; Home → Program → Log was three steps for the most frequent action.

2

Check-in is a bottom sheet, from Home or Program

It's three taps of input. A full screen push added weight and an extra back-stack entry.

3

Preview + Save removed

The program saves when generated and lands on Program, nothing auto-started. A first-visit "Redo the questions" covers "this isn't right".

4

Exercise Detail becomes a sheet; levels expand in place

Keeps the user's place in the list and removes a screen from the stack.

5

No bottom bar yet

With two destinations, tabs would be thin. Home gets a settings button; the layout leaves room for a later Today · Progress · Exercises · Ask bar, hidden on immersive screens.

6

Name on first launch (skippable)

Lets Home greet by name. Stays on the phone, no account.

7

Gentle day (flare-up) sheet

Gives "lower levels never disappear" a real everyday use: on a bad day, a short Level 1 set is one tap from Home.

8

Region as tiles; questions get one calm intro line each where it matters

Less form-like, and the warning-sign question is introduced as a routine check instead of arriving out of nowhere.

9

"How it felt" leads; pain is shown quietly

Charting pain closely can make people watch it more. A soft dot strip with a plain sentence reflects it without turning it into a score.

10

Settings (new, not urgent)

Opt-in reminders, export (no accounts means a lost phone loses everything), delete all data, About.

11

No gym tracking

It pulls the app toward "gym", competes with dedicated trackers, and most users aren't lifting. At most, an optional "Also moved today" on the check-in.

Found in backIntake.ts: review before building

The diagnosed branch never reaches the warning-sign checklist: diagnosisDetail → clearedForExercise → location → aggravating → current pain → COMPLETE. Someone with a diagnosis and new bladder or bowel changes would get a program.

Also, "No / not yet" cleared for exercise continues to a program rather than the doctor screen. Worth confirming that's intended with whoever audits the questions.

Rules for the build (not visible in mockups)

• Re-show the warning-sign question when "Significant" pain is logged twice in a row (agreed).

• Gentle-day sessions are logged and shown, but don't count toward or against level readiness.

• When "Not yet" is re-offered is your call; the design doesn't depend on it.

• Questions are presented differently but their wording and order are untouched. Intro lines sit above questions, never inside them.
